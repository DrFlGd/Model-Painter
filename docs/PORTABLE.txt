Model Painter (portable)

Nothing to install: keep this folder wherever you like and run
"Model Painter.exe" from it. It keeps nothing beside itself; the models you
paint are saved where you choose.

Open a model with "Open a model…" or by dropping it (or its folder) on the
window: its textures are found beside it, in its folder or the folders near
it (a "textures" folder next to the model's, say).

Windows: SmartScreen may say the app is unrecognised, because it is not
signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". Windows 10 and 11 already
have WebView2, which the app uses; on an older Windows install it from
Microsoft (search "WebView2 runtime").
