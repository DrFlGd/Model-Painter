The first Model Painter: open a model with its textures, paint it for colour printing from the textures or by hand, and save a painted 3MF.

**Download**: `ModelPainter_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` installs it; `ModelPainter_0.1.0_x64-portable.zip` runs wherever you unzip it.

- **Opens** glTF/GLB, OBJ, FBX, Collada, 3DS, PLY, PSK/PSKX, STL and 3MF, with their textures (DDS including BC7, PNG, TGA, JPEG, WebP, BMP). Textures are found beside the model, in its folder or the ones near it; a missing one can be chosen by hand. A painted 3MF (this app's, Dark STL's, or a slicer project) comes back with its paint and colours.
- **Print size**: set the height or width in millimetres and turn the model a quarter at a time. Game models in metres or centimetres are sized to 150 mm tall to start with.
- **Paint from textures**: Dark STL's texture painting, with the colours chosen from every texture by how much of the model each texel covers. **Prepare texture** shows a texture beside what painting will make of it, with the colour count, merging and leaving out colours, a chip per colour to keep, drop or change, smoothing, specks, exposure, contrast, saturation and the smallest feature.
- **Paint by hand** (from Gus Gus Goose and Print Factory): up to 16 colours; brush, fill up to creases, corners, text, picture stamps through **Prepare image**, and pick a colour from the model; undo and redo.
- **Save** a painted 3MF for Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer (with a picture for the slicer's file list), a 3MF split into a solid part per colour (for a closed model), or an STL.
