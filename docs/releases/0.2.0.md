Dark STL's finishing comes to Model Painter: a **Finish** tab makes a game model one watertight solid for printing, and keeps its paint and textures on it.

**Download**: `ModelPainter_0.2.0_x64-setup.exe` installs it; `ModelPainter_0.2.0_x64-portable.zip` runs wherever you unzip it.

- **Make watertight**: each part of the model is made solid (closed as it is, small openings such as a neck or a sleeve end closed flat, open sheets such as capes thickened to a set thickness, broken parts rebuilt from voxels), then the parts are joined into one solid with only the outside left, as Dark STL does. On a Bloodborne model it finds the same parts as Dark STL and the same volume within 0.1 %.
  - **Fill hollows**: space sealed inside the model (between a body and a coat over it) is filled.
  - **Close narrow gaps**: slits up to a width you set (a sleeve round an arm) are bridged under the surface.
  - **Remove loose bits**: separate pieces smaller than a share of the model go.
- **Paint and textures come along**: what is still the model's surface keeps its paint exactly (split triangles' paint is copied, or repainted from the paint over the piece where finishing cut it) and its UVs, so the textures show as before and **Paint from textures** works on it. Surfaces finishing made (lids over openings, the backs of thickened sheets, rebuilt parts, bridges) take their colour and texture from the nearest of the model facing the same way.
- **Stand it on the plate**: cut the bottom flat by a set amount, and add a round or square base under the feet (thickness and how far past the feet), painted colour 1.
- **Original and Watertight** are both kept, each with its own paint and size; switch between them on the Finish tab. The one shown is the one painted and saved. The tab says when the watertight one is out of date (the original's size, turn or paint changed, or the settings did).
- The Finish tab says how far the model is from a closed solid (open edges, edges shared by more than two triangles).
- Parts that touch along an edge (two boxes sharing one) are taken apart there and stay as they are, where Dark STL rebuilds them from voxels; parts rebuilt from voxels are simplified to under half the triangles without visible change.
- The README's steps were reordered (Model, Finish, Textures, Paint, Save).
