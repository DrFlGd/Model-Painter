Dark STL's cutting comes to Model Painter: a **Cut** tab cuts a model into pieces that fit your printer, joined by pegs, dowels, magnets or a key, and each piece keeps its paint.

**Download**: `ModelPainter_0.3.0_x64-setup.exe` installs it; `ModelPainter_0.3.0_x64-portable.zip` runs wherever you unzip it.

- **Place the cuts in the view**: add a cut across X, Y or Z, or facing you. Each is a see-through square through the model's middle: drag it to slide it, drag its round handle to tilt it, or **Pick a point** on the model for it to go through. The tab has the same as a position slider, tilt and turn.
- **A joint for each cut**, with its own clearance:
  - **Flat**: the cut faces as they are, to glue.
  - **Pegs**: round pegs on one side and holes on the other, wider and deeper by the clearance. Diameter, depth, how many (automatic or 1 to 4), and which side.
  - **Dowels**: holes on both sides, and the dowels as pieces too (or not, for metal pins or filament).
  - **Magnets**: round pockets on both sides for disc magnets.
  - **Key**: a tongue in the shape of the cut face into a groove, so the pieces only fit one way.
  - **Use this joint for every cut** copies it to the others.
- **Joints placed with care**: pegs, dowels and magnets go on each separate area of the cut face (a leg and a sleeve each get their own), as far from the edge as they can, keeping a **wall around joints** (1.6 mm, under More settings). One that would break through the far side of a piece is made shorter or left out, and the tab says how many places were left flat.
- **The pieces**: shown in colours or in their paint, spread apart by a slider, with each piece's size, triangles and volume. Moving a cut cuts again quickly: the model is made solid once.
- **Paint survives**: the pieces keep the model's paint, UVs and materials; the cut faces, pegs and holes are colour 1.
- **Either model**: the watertight model (Finish tab) is cut as it is; the original is made solid first, as Make watertight does.
- **Save each piece**: under Save, **Each piece** writes every piece to its own painted 3MF, 3MF split by colour or STL, in a new folder `<name>_pieces` (a zip in a browser). **Lay the pieces flat to print** puts each on its biggest cut face without pegs or a tongue, centred, and stands dowels on end; off, they keep their place in the model.
- Checked against Dark STL: its test figure cut with pegs, dowels and a key gives the same pieces and volumes; a Bloodborne model of 30 parts cut with pegs gives the same pieces and joints, with volumes within 0.6 % (one piece holding a part rebuilt from voxels differs by 1.1 %, within what Dark STL itself gives that part with other random seeds).
