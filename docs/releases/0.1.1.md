Jerred's first fixes: moving round the model while painting, picking a colour without opening the colour picker, and more ways to fill.

**Download**: `ModelPainter_0.1.1_x64-setup.exe` installs it; `ModelPainter_0.1.1_x64-portable.zip` runs wherever you unzip it.

- **Move the view** in every tab: drag with the middle button, hold Space and drag, Shift with the turning button, or turn on the Move button beside Fit (H, Esc turns it off) so the left button slides the view. The wheel zooms towards what is under the mouse. A line at the top of the view says which button does what.
- **Colours**: clicking a colour picks it to paint with. The arrow beside it opens the colour picker.
- **Fill** has four ways to spread, kept between runs:
  - **Same colour**: the connected area in the colour you click, whatever its shape, so one painted area can be repainted without changing that colour anywhere else.
  - **Up to creases**: as before, stopping at edges sharper than an angle.
  - **Flat area**: stopping where the surface turns more than an angle from where you click, for a face with rounded edges that a crease fill leaks over.
  - **Whole part**: every triangle joined to the one you click, whatever its colour.
- Fills on texture-painted models are much faster: they follow the paint piece by piece instead of opening every split triangle the colour touches (a fill on a Bloodborne model took 1.1 s, now 40 ms).
- Number keys and tool keys work after clicking a slider or a fill option.
